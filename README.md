A Python program that draws a complete newspaper front page — "The World Today", Friday, 20 November 2026 — entirely in code with Pillow, and writes it to /output/entry.png. The lead story is the International Court of Justice ruling, 9-6, that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, the first time the World Court has held a state responsible for genocide. Below it: Russia's overnight barrage of 110 missiles and some 400 drones on Kyiv hours after the Shenzhen summit (nine killed, 28 injured, three bridges damaged); Trump, Xi and Putin's first three-way summit in Shenzhen and its four-point declaration; and Anthropic's record $81.3bn Nasdaq debut, closing 17% above its offer price while the S&P 500 fell 2% and the Nikkei dropped 4.8%. A briefs strip adds the Houthi missile strike on Riyadh, COP31's dropped fossil-fuel phase-out, and Kenya's last Ebola patient discharged. No external images, no network, no randomness: two runs write byte-identical PNGs (1024x1024, under 1 MiB).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
